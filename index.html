About Us

Shopping decisions, made easier.

ValueD is a product price comparison website for shoppers in India. It puts offers for the same product from different retailers side by side, so you can compare them in one place instead of piecing them together yourself.

Where ValueD is today

  • This version is a front-end demonstration that uses clearly labelled sample data.
  • It does not show live or verified prices, and it does not sell products.
  • There are no user accounts, and price-drop alerts are not available.

Why compare prices

The price, delivery terms and availability of the same product can differ from one retailer to another. Seeing them together helps you judge which offer suits you.

Our mission

To make shopping decisions easier by bringing comparable offer information into one clear, easy-to-read place.

Our vision

Transparent product-price comparisons: clear about what is being shown, where it comes from and how current it is.

Our commitment

To help you make informed decisions: we label sample or unverified information clearly, avoid promising savings, and leave the final choice with you.
